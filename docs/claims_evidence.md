# Claims & Evidence appendix

The Gilded Ace, Jan 1 to Jun 30 2025. One row per claim shown in the [README](../README.md) or on a dashboard page. Each row names the table or query behind the number, the result, the alternative explanation that was tested, and the main limitation. Details, reproduction code and reconciliation checks live in the linked `findings/` file.

**Review status.** AGENTS.md requires that a claim used in the README or slides comes from a finding whose `Checked by` is not `unchecked`. At the time of writing (2026-11-07) every finding below is still `unchecked`: each was verified by its author against raw data with independent SQL, but no second person has signed it off. The `Checked by` column mirrors the finding file and must be updated here when a reviewer signs a finding. Until then every claim is provisional.

Conventions used throughout: money math goes through `royale/money.py`; ratios are ratios of sums; theo = coin-in x (1 - configured RTP); luck residual = house win - theo; machine comparisons exclude the 1,018 off-floor `machine_day` rows (`location_id IS NULL`); scheduled hours come from `calendar_day.scheduled_seconds` (Mar 8 has 23 h); unusual payouts are inspection leads, never accusations.

## How the claims fit together

The manager's list of the 10 lowest earners is the baseline for every machine claim. Adjusting for exposure and luck keeps 8 of them in the bottom 10 (M1). Of the two that leave, M-1158 is the single machine whose payouts sit outside the configured expectation (M2), and seven picks share a low-traffic Back Forty location that lost about 30% of its demand while its walkway was closed (M3, D1). Downtime is real but small and concentrated in Ridgeline fault frequency (T1 to T3), so it does not explain the bottom 10. The July plan therefore inspects M-1158 before any replacement and preserves the rest of the budget (A1, A2). Player, comp and forecast claims (P1, C1, C2, F1, F2) describe the floor's guests and demand and are not inputs to the machine decision.
