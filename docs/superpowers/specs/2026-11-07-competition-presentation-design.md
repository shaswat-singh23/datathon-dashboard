# Competition presentation design

## Intent

Create a polished competition demo with investor-style storytelling, as clarified by the user. The audience should understand the decision, see compelling evidence, and explore the analysis confidently. This is a synthetic-data casino case study, not a fundraising pitch or proof of commercial traction.

## Recommended direction

Keep Streamlit, the existing eight routes, committed marts, and luxury casino identity. Rework the information hierarchy and decision visuals before adding more decoration. Give the home page a distinctive illustrated identity; use smaller branded headers on analytical pages. All six challenge questions, model validation, source evidence, and priced recommendations remain reachable.

Alternatives considered: a separate slide-like website would duplicate the evidence and introduce deployment work; a complete frontend rewrite would add risk without improving the underlying story. A focused refinement of the existing dashboard is the recommended approach, subject to the user's review of this plan.

## Experience

The narrative is **a costly instinct → competing explanations → an evidence-led decision → a way to verify it**. Home presents the decision and three evidence cards. Machines explains the change in rankings; Demand shows the closure comparison; Actions shows the price and assumptions; Model demonstrates honest validation. Players, Comps, and Downtime remain prominent in exploration.

## Constraints

- Fictional casino and synthetic data must remain visibly disclosed.
- Budget preserved is not savings, profit, or measured return on investment.
- Observational associations and hypothetical recovery scenarios must remain identified.
- The main write-up stays at or below 400 words; the submitted video stays at or below 120 seconds.
- Keep existing routes and numerical definitions; presentation changes must not silently change populations or calculations.
- Maintain accessible contrast, keyboard controls, and responsive layouts. Decorative art must not obstruct reading or interaction.
- Keep the local dashboard running for the user's exploration. Implementation and publication are separate from this planning task.

## Success

A first-time viewer can identify the decision in 15 seconds, trace it to evidence in two clicks, and complete the demonstrated story in approximately 110 seconds. These are proposed acceptance targets, not measured results. The app must still support all four judge tasks: period change, bank/guest drill, model validation, and recommendation tracing.
