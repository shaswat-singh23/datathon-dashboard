# The Gilded Ace: investigate before replacing

Management plans to replace its ten lowest-earning casino machines. Using the fictional Gilded Ace's January–June 2025 records, we ask whether weak earnings reflect exposure, downtime, payout luck or location before committing to replacements.

Our Streamlit dashboard supports period changes, bank and guest-group drills, model validation and recommendation tracing. We compare the manager's raw house-win ranking with expected contribution per available hour, follow dated placements and exclude off-floor records. Rates divide summed amounts by summed exposure. [Eight of the manager's ten picks remain in the adjusted bottom ten](https://github.com/shaswat-singh23/datathon-dashboard/blob/master/findings/22-machines.md); rankings do not establish replacement benefits.

The headline is an access lead: across five-week before/during windows, Back Forty coin-in per available machine-hour fell from **$135.5 to $96.3**, while the rest of the floor went from **$206.1 to $210.7**. This [closure comparison](https://github.com/shaswat-singh23/datathon-dashboard/blob/master/findings/16-demand-changes.md) is observational; renovation and machine mix remain alternative explanations. Payout anomalies likewise warrant inspection, not accusations.

For daily coin-in forecasting, we train on January–April and freeze predictions for May–June. Holdout mean absolute error is **$218,258/day** for the constant training mean, **$68,699/day** for weekday means and **$96,658/day** for fitted weekday-plus-trend regression. The [weekday baseline wins this holdout](https://github.com/shaswat-singh23/datathon-dashboard/blob/master/findings/47-forecast-evaluation.md); future superiority is unproven. Inputs use calendar dates only, excluding later-published context.

Our [July 1–30 plan](https://github.com/shaswat-singh23/datathon-dashboard/blob/master/findings/23-july-actions.md) funds one payout/configuration inspection of M-1158 at **$1,200**: **total spend $1,200**, preserving **$98,800**. Preventive maintenance (**$9,000**) and extra grave-shift technician coverage (**$11,520**) are deferred alternatives. Preserving all funds wins the monetary comparison. Inspection buys information; no positive revenue recovery is estimated. Recovery ranges are scenarios, not confidence intervals; inspection downtime also carries an opportunity cost.

Verify configuration by July 2, then monitor availability and variance-standardized payout residuals through July 30 against same-configuration controls and June records. Any correction needs a separate effect estimate; regression to the mean is not an inspection benefit.
