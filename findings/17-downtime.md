# Downtime: 5,021 machine-hours lost, ~$974k coin-in (~$91k theo); Ridgeline faults 2.9x as often
- Issue: #17 | Author: Alex (dandelino-del, with Claude Code) | Time: 12:54
- Claim: Jan 1 to Jun 30 2025, on-floor machines lost **5,021 machine-hours** to downtime, **0.38%** of 1,321,908 scheduled hours. At each machine's own same-month coin-in per available hour, that is an estimated **$973,564 coin-in** and **$90,663 theo** (expected house win; 0.39% of the period's $23.55M theo). The worst manufacturer is **Ridgeline**: 0.68% of scheduled hours lost (2,755 h across 94 machines, median 28 h per machine) vs Northwind 0.30% and Halcyon 0.20%. The cause is how often they fault, not how slowly they are fixed: **1.68 faults per 1,000 scheduled hours vs 0.57 for the rest of the fleet (rate ratio 2.93)**, with the same median repair response (3.2 to 3.5 h). The 10 machines with the most lost hours are all Ridgeline. Worst bank: **B-24, 0.71%** lost (bank range 0.04% to 0.71%, median 0.38%). Worst zone: Bar Row 0.52%; High-Limit Room is lowest at 0.08%. Repair speed varies by shift: faults opened on grave (22:00-06:00) wait **1.96 h** on average for a tech vs 0.87 h on day and swing. Grave has 1.0 slot tech on duty on average vs 1.5 on day and 1.9 on swing.
- Evidence:
  - Totals, month, manufacturer, zone, bank: `uv run python -m royale.build q3_downtime`, then from `io.mart("q3_downtime")` where `record_type == "machine_day"`: `SUM(lost_h)`, `SUM(lost_h)/SUM(scheduled_h)`, `SUM(lost_coin_in_usd)`, `SUM(lost_theo_usd)` grouped by `manufacturer`, `zone_name`, `bank_id`. Mart SQL: `royale/marts/q3_downtime.py` (lost_h = `calendar_day.scheduled_seconds - machine_day.available_seconds`, location_id not null; rate = `SUM(coin_in)/SUM(available_h)` per machine x month).
  - By month (lost hrs / share): Jan 867 / 0.39%, Feb 772 / 0.38%, Mar 979 / 0.44%, Apr 755 / 0.34%, May 1,017 / 0.44%, Jun 630 / 0.28%.
  - Fault rate: `record_type == "ticket" AND event_type == "fault_repair"` counts per manufacturer (Halcyon 222, Northwind 305, Ridgeline 680) / `SUM(scheduled_h)` per manufacturer. Ridgeline vs rest, conditional binomial test of the Poisson rate ratio: p ≈ 1e-76. Response time by manufacturer, Kruskal-Wallis: p = 0.49 (no difference).
  - Shift wait: mean `wait_h` (work_started_at - opened_at) by `shift`: day 0.90, swing 0.85, grave 1.96 h (n = 327 / 577 / 303). Grave vs others, one-sided Mann-Whitney: p ≈ 1e-13. Staffing: `SELECT shift, avg(techs) FROM (SELECT gaming_date, shift, sum(actual_headcount) techs FROM shift_roster WHERE role='slot_tech' GROUP BY 1,2) GROUP BY 1` gives day 1.48, swing 1.92, grave 1.00.
  - Baseline: the fleet-wide lost share (0.38%) and fault rate (0.57 per 1,000 h excluding Ridgeline) are the comparison for every group.
- So what: Downtime costs roughly $15k of expected house win a month, real but small next to $23.5M of theo, so it does not explain a machine landing in the bottom 10 unless that machine is a Ridgeline with heavy fault days. When the machines page ranks per available hour, Ridgeline machines get an exposure correction their raw totals hide. The lever is fault frequency on Ridgeline (service contract, preventive maintenance) and grave-shift tech coverage, not general repair speed.
- Alternative explanations / limitations: Lost coin-in assumes a lost hour would play like the machine's average available hour that month. Faults on busy evenings understate it, overnight faults overstate it, and guests may move to a nearby machine. `available_seconds` subtracts moves and installs as well as faults (repairs dominate: 1,207 of 1,226 tickets). Ridgeline machines may sit in higher-traffic spots that wear them faster; their zone mix was not controlled for here. The shift-staffing link is correlational.
